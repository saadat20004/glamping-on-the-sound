A little slice of heaven by the water

Bring your tent and set up camp right beside the water. You can even arrive by boat and pitch your tent the moment you step ashore.

Enjoy the deck, dock, fire pit, picnic table, outdoor shower and Blackstone grill. Bring a fishing pole, use the two kayaks, and get in and out of the water easily with the Wet Steps.

You also have the remodeled kitchen, a bathroom, washer and dryer, and a golf cart to explore Cobb Island and its nearby restaurants.
